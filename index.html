About Me

I am an Adolf Martens Fellow at the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing (BAM), Berlin, Germany. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Université Laval, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Xavier Maldague. I am Member of IEEE, SPIE, ASME, ASNT, and CINDE. I am Guest Editor for Journal of Applied Physics and Sensors Journal.

My research lies at the intersection of photothermal imaging, terahertz imaging, AI for science, theoretical physics, quantum physics, inverse problems, and non-destructive evaluation. I develop physics-informed learning methods and advanced imaging techniques to reveal hidden structural, thermal, and material information in complex systems.

My current research focuses on virtual wave transform, physics-informed neural networks, neural operators, photothermal, terahertz, quantum physics, heat transfer, and super-resolution imaging.

Research Interests

Scientific Machine Learning

Physics-informed neural networks, super-resolution networks, Fourier neural operators, cross-physics neural operators, and AI for scientific discovery.

Photothermal Imaging

Optical-excited thermography, structural-illumination imaging, thermal diffusivity measurement, vibrothermography, and virtual wave imaging.

Terahertz Imaging and Spectroscopy

THz-TDS, terahertz tomography, material characterization, super-resolution imaging, and molecular energy-level difference spectrum.

Diffusion-Wave Physics

Spectral-domain diffusion-wave theory, spectral lifting, group theory, unified operator theory, spectral Snell’s law, and inverse problems in diffusive systems.
