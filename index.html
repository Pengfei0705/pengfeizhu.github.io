About Me

I am an Adolf Martens Fellow at the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing (BAM), Berlin, Germany. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Université Laval, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Xavier Maldague. I am a member of IEEE, SPIE, ASME, ASNT, and CINDE, and serve as a Guest Editor for Journal of Applied Physics and IEEE Sensors Journal.

My research lies at the intersection of photothermal imaging, terahertz imaging, AI for science, theoretical physics, quantum physics, inverse problems, and non-destructive evaluation. I develop physics-informed learning methods and advanced imaging techniques to reveal hidden structural, thermal, and material information in complex systems.

My current research focuses on virtual-wave transformation, physics-informed learning, neural operators, photothermal and terahertz imaging, heat transport, quantum-inspired formulations, and computational super-resolution.

Research Interests

Physics-Informed Learning

Physics-informed neural networks, super-resolution networks, cross-physics neural operators, and AI for scientific discovery.

Photothermal Imaging

Optical-excited thermography, structural-illumination imaging, thermal diffusivity measurement, vibrothermography, and virtual wave imaging.

Terahertz Imaging and Spectroscopy

THz-TDS, terahertz tomography, material characterization, super-resolution THz imaging, and molecular energy-level difference spectrum.

Diffusion-Wave Physics

Diffusion-wave theory, spectral geometry, group theory, unified operator theory, spectral Snell’s law, and inverse problems in diffusive systems.
